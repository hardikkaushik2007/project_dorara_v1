01 // ARCHITECTURAL WALKTHROUGH

How Dora solves the app discovery paradox.

Instead of forcing users to know every tool beforehand, Dora acts as an informed companion who finds the right tool and helps you use it.

01VOICE INTENT EXTRACTION

Speak in natural English or Hinglish.

Say 'Dora, mujhe AI ki help se research karni hai' or 'Find me an AI tool for healthcare research.' Dora decomposes your request into domain constraints, academic rigor requirements, and platform targets.

02EVIDENCE-BASED DISCOVERY

Unmatched verified curation over random web search.

Dora indexes verified capabilities across 20+ applications with audited pricing, Android package targets, and real-time limitation disclaimers. No fabricated ratings or hallucinated features.

03CAPABILITY-DRIVEN LAUNCH

Safe execution with honest guidance fallback.

Dora launches verified HTTPS custom tabs or installed apps. When third-party apps restrict automated form submission, Dora stages the prompt, copies it to clipboard, and guides you step-by-step.

04 // CAPABILITY & TRANSPARENCY

Built on technical honesty.

No universal automation illusions. A disciplined capability matrix designed to keep users informed and in control.

// CAPABILITY_1

Honest State Verification

REQUESTED -> ATTEMPTED -> COMPLETED -> VERIFIED

Dora refuses to claim an action has succeeded until observable criteria confirm it. Launching a browser window does not pretend the user's research is finished.

// CAPABILITY_2

Zero Keystroke Injection Abuse

PERMISSION_MODEL: EXPLICIT_CONSENT

No intrusive AccessibilityService permission requests during onboarding. Dora formats and stages your query, then guides you with zero background tracking.

// CAPABILITY_3

Dual Operation Mode

OFFLINE_FIRST + CLOUD_ENHANCED

Works 100% offline with an embedded Android catalogue and on-device NLP. When connected to the cloud, it accesses real-time ranking and index synchronization.
